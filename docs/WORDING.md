# Burn by Dawn — the wording sheet

Every line the player sees, grouped by where it shows, for rewording.

**How to use it.** Change the words after the `|` and save. Leave everything before the
`|` alone: the tag (A1, B2…) is how your change finds its way back into the game, and
any words beside it (a mission, a man, a lesson step) only say which one it is. To cut a
line, write CUT after the `|`. To say something about a line rather than reword it, add
`(note: …)` after it.

**‹Words in angle brackets›** are filled in by the game: a number, a man, a place. Keep
them, move them, or drop them. The one shown is an example.

A `/` between two phrases means the game shows one or the other, depending on the moment.

Made 17 Oct 2026 (build M53). The game is unchanged until your rewording is carried back.
