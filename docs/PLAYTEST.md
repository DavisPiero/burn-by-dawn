# Burn by Dawn — v1.0 playtest sheet

For the operator. This is the gate to v1.0 (ROADMAP.md): a round with **3–5 people new to
the game**, the game working in **Chrome, Safari and Firefox**, and no rules changes asked
for. Print one copy of "Per player" for each person.

Game: https://davispiero.github.io/burn-by-dawn/ — check the build line in the left
margin reads the build you mean to test before anyone sits down.

---

## Before they start

- A desktop or laptop with a mouse, the window at least 1280 × 800. No phones or tablets.
- Sound on, at a comfortable level (the sounds are part of the feel).
- Say only this: *"It's a turn-based game about six paratroopers blowing a bridge at
  night. I can't help once you start. Please think out loud."* Nothing about the rules,
  the keys or what to do first. The game has to teach itself.
- Let them choose the level. Note which one they pick and why.
- Don't help. If they are truly stuck for more than a minute, note what they were stuck
  on, then say just enough to unstick them, and mark it **HELPED**.

## What to watch

Write down the moment, not your view of it ("turn 4, tried to click Dutch's portrait to
move him"). The questions below say what each watch is for.

1. **The orders page.** Do they read it or skip it? Do they find the difficulty and the
   counter key?
2. **The drop.** Do they understand they are choosing a run? Which run, and why? Do they
   read the tags (QUIET / STEADY / FAST)? *Open question: does West still deserve QUIET
   now the Wood patrol walks it?*
3. **The first turn after landing.** Do they pack parachutes? Did Dutch's "Pack the chutes
   boys!" land, or did they only find out when a patrol found a chute?
4. **Hover.** Do they discover the hover readout and the red risk dots without being told?
   How long before they do?
5. **Hunter and hunted.** Count the knife kills and gunner kills. Do they go hunting,
   hide, or both? Does killing feel worth a whole turn of that man? Does any turn feel like
   "nothing useful to do"?
6. **Charges.** Do they find Pick up charge and Pass charge when a carrier is hit? Do they
   see the red prompt for Stabilise or Pass?
7. **The alert dial.** Do they notice it rise? Do they call the RAF diversion, and when?
8. **The exfil.** Do they know where it is and when to go? Any surprise at the end?
9. **Where they get confused, bored or annoyed.** Note the turn and what was on screen.
10. **Where they grin, swear or lean in.** The same.

## After the game (5 minutes, ask in this order)

1. What happened, in your own words?
2. What was the best moment? The most frustrating?
3. Was there anything you wanted to do and couldn't find?
4. Did any of the six men stand out? Who, and why?
5. Would you play again? At the same level, or a different one?
6. Did the game feel fair when things went wrong?

Don't defend the game or explain it until they've answered everything.

are enough for Claude to reproduce it.

## What counts as passing the gate

- Nobody asks for a rules change. (Polish, wording and art notes are fine: they go in a
  review milestone as usual.)
- The browser check is all ticks in all three.
- A player new to the game wins **Normal within three tries**, and Hard feels like the
  real test (SPEC.md §10, Balance targets).

Bring the sheets back to Claude as they are: the notes matter more than the tidy answers.
Then tag v1.0.
